Computational Modeling

Using simulation to study materials, circuits, and electrical behavior.

SPH Fiber Suspension

I am modeling 3D printing fiber suspension using smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) in Python, mentored by Professor Gokul Gopal. My work contributes to an ongoing research study of 3D printer nozzle geometries to improve fiber orientation and print quality.

Current/Voltage Modeling

With what I learned from my SPH project, I’m applying those concepts to begin modeling current and electron flow.

RC Circuit Simulator

Developed an interactive Python-based simulator to model the behavior of resistor-capacitor (RC) circuits. Users can adjust resistance, capacitance, and input voltage to visualize how capacitor voltage and current change during charging and discharging. The simulator plots voltage and current over time, demonstrating concepts such as exponential decay, time constants, and transient circuit behavior.
