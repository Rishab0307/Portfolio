Research

Machine learning research on static and dynamic IR drop detection.

Static IR Drop Detection

Under the mentorship of Stanford researcher Abdulla Kerimov, PhD, I developed and evaluated eight Python/TensorFlow machine learning architectures: five transfer learning models and three models trained from scratch. Using the open source CircuitNet dataset, I conducted more than 10 literature reviews and ran tests comparing the two approaches. My Feature Pyramid Network (FPN) model achieved 0.92 Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM). My paper has been submitted for publication to The National High School Journal of Science (NHSJS).

Dynamic IR Drop

An extension of my earlier project, now in progress. This work studies IR drop as a time dependent problem. My previous work condensed time into an average; now each time step is an input the model needs to consider.
