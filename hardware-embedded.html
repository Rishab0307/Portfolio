Hardware & Embedded Systems

Hands-on electronics, sensing, and embedded builds.

Voltage Droop PCB

I built a custom PCB based power delivery analyzer using an ESP32 and current and voltage sensing to measure how voltage changes under different loads. The project connects hardware measurements to concepts like IR drop, resistance, and power delivery.

Arduino RC Car

I used Bluetooth to pair two HC-05 modules: a controller and a worker. I connected a joystick to the controller and motors and wheels to the worker, then coded the joystick to control the motors. I mounted the worker setup on a 3D printed car chassis to make a working RC car.
