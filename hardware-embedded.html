Hardware & Embedded Systems

Electronics, circuit behavior, and digital systems.

Voltage Droop PCB

I built a custom PCB based power delivery analyzer using an ESP32 and current and voltage sensing to measure how voltage changes under different loads. The project connects hardware measurements to concepts like IR drop, resistance, and power delivery.

Digital Logic Simulator

Created a Python-based simulator to demonstrate fundamental digital logic gates and Boolean operations. Users can interact with AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR gates by changing binary inputs and observing the outputs. The simulator generates truth tables and supports combining gates into simple digital circuits, helping visualize how binary inputs are processed through logical operations.
