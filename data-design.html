Vector & Matrix Transformation Visualizer

Built an interactive Python-based visualizer to explore vector operations and 2D matrix transformations. Users can perform vector addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, dot products, and projections while viewing the results on a coordinate plane. The program also visualizes how 2×2 matrices transform vectors and geometric shapes through rotations, reflections, scaling, and shearing.
